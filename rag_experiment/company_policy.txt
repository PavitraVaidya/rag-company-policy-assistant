Company Leave Policy

1. Employees receive 18 days of paid leave per calendar year.
2. Employees can carry forward a maximum of 5 unused leave days to the next year.
3. Employees must submit leave requests at least 3 working days in advance.
4. Sick leave lasting more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
5. Employees must contact the HR department to request emergency leave.
6. The company office is located in Bengaluru.
7. New employees become eligible for paid leave after completing 30 days of employment.
